About Otto

What Otto is

Each morning, tell me what I owe people and whether I can take on more — without digging through Slack, email, and Teams.

A personal work tracker fed by an AI scan, not manual entry. It reads your Slack, email, Teams, and calendar and stages what needs follow-up into a review queue.

Getting started

  1. Run the otto-scan skill in Cowork — it reads Slack, email, Teams, and your calendar.
  2. Back here, click the sync button (top-right) to pull in what it found. ← the step people forget.
  3. New items wait in the Review tab — approve the keepers, reject the noise. Nothing hits your board until you do.
  4. Triage as normal, and check the capacity gauge before taking on more.
Looks broken, isn’t:
  • After reloading the page, you’ll see an amber “Reconnect & load” bar and the sync button lit up — just click it. Your data is safe in the file; browsers drop the file link on reload by design.
  • Scanned but nothing new showed up? You haven’t hit sync yet (step 2).

First time here? That same sync button is where you link your otto-data.json file — see the handoff guide for one-time setup.

What it does

  • Scan → Review — scanned items stage in a queue; you approve or reject, so nothing lands unreviewed.
  • Today · This week · Later — tasks bucketed by when you’ll do them.
  • Follow-ups — open conversations that need an action, so they don’t slip.
  • Teams — notes and recaps from recorded meetings; action items flow into Review.
  • Capacity gauge — meeting hours + task hours against your daily ceiling, so you know if there’s room.
  • Staleness nudges — items you’ve sat on get flagged, and clicking the tag resets the clock.
  • Snooze — push a follow-up out to tomorrow, a few days, or next week.
  • Resolution & Archive — close things as done, reassigned, or dropped; nothing is ever deleted.
  • Weekly review — a step-back on what closed, what’s aging, and the week’s meeting load.

What it’s deliberately not

  • Not a generic to-do app — it’s a sharp tool for one job, not a catch-all.
  • Not a team tool — single-user and private; there’s no shared or cross-person view.
  • No sub-tasks, dependencies, time tracking, pomodoro, gamification, or noisy notifications.
  • It won’t add anything to your board on its own — scanned items always wait for your review.

Why it’s built this way

  • A sharp tool beats a bloated one — every “nice to have” is resisted on purpose.
  • Capacity is real daily hours, not abstract points — it maps to your actual day.
  • Deferred isn’t forgotten — Later and snoozed items are exempt from staleness.
  • Nothing is ever deleted — rejected and resolved items stay searchable in case they boomerang back.
